Privacy Policy

SystemDevtools for iPad and iPhone · Effective 23 November 2026

SystemDevtools does not collect, store, or share any personal data with the developer or with third parties. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

What stays on your device

The servers you add (name, address, port, username, and pinned host key) and your display settings are stored on your device. SSH private keys and passwords are stored in your device's Keychain and are available only on that device. A key created in the app lives in the Secure Enclave and can never be exported. Like other app data, host records and settings may be included in your device backups.

Network connections

The app connects only to the servers you add, over SSH, to read system statistics and to send the process actions you confirm. That data goes straight from your server to your device. It is not sent anywhere else. iOS may ask for permission to reach servers on your local network.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
